New: Writing check, a grammar and spelling check that runs on your phone. Tap its keyboard button to review what you typed one suggestion at a time, each with a reason, or paste text into the Writing check screen. Set it as Android's spell checker to see mistakes underlined in other apps, whatever keyboard you use. It checks spelling against a dictionary, subject-verb agreement, missing commas and common mix-ups, and learns words you keep. Nothing you type leaves the phone.
