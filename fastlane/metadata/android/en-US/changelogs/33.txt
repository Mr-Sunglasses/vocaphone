Cancel dictation while you are still speaking: the keyboard X now shows during Listening. Leaving a field mid-dictation saves the transcript to History instead of losing it, and failures say what went wrong and offer Retry. Pick a maximum recording length from 1 to 10 minutes (5 by default). Model downloads resume after a dropped connection. Also new: long-press space to switch keyboards, a setting to turn off auto-capitalization, and Traditional Chinese for Taiwan and Hong Kong.
