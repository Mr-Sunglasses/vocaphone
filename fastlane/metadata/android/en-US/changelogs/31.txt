VocaPhoneX joins the family: a separate sideload build that puts a floating mic bubble over any app — tap a text field, tap the bubble, dictate, and the transcript lands in the field, no keyboard switch needed. Every model, gateway, style, and snippets setting works the same. This release also makes dictation cancellation instant and reliable, keeps the microphone alive across queued dictations, and fixes retries from the bubble going to the wrong place.
